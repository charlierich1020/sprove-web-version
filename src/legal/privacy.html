Sporv is a marketplace where families find and book youth-sports providers. This notice explains what we collect, why, who it reaches, and what you can ask us to do about it.

1. Who we are

Sporv Inc., Chicago, Illinois, USA. For any privacy question, or to exercise a right described below, email support@sporv.ai.

2. What we collect

  • Account information — name, email address, mobile number where you give one, and the role you use the service in.
  • Athlete information — provided by a parent or guardian: first name, age or date of birth, sport, and anything they choose to add about goals or needs.
  • Booking and payment information — sessions booked, amounts, and payment status. Card numbers go directly to Stripe; Sporv never stores them.
  • Messages — what you send through the service to a provider or a family.
  • Technical information — IP address, device and browser type, and pages requested.

3. SMS Communications

Sporv sends text messages to parents and guardians on behalf of sports organizations. Messages include schedule changes, cancellations, payment reminders, and registration notices. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe, HELP for help. We do not sell phone numbers.

Two additions to the above, because campaign review asks for them explicitly: mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages, and the mobile number and the opt-in consent you give are never shared with any third party for that party's own marketing. A number you provide is passed only to the messaging provider that delivers the message on our behalf, and only for that purpose. Stopping SMS does not close your account; you will still receive email about your bookings.

4. Why we use it

To create and secure accounts; to show relevant listings; to take a booking and move payment; to let a family and a provider communicate about a session; to send transactional email and SMS; to detect fraud and abuse; and to meet legal obligations. We do not use personal information to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell personal information.

5. Who it reaches

Personal data reaches the subprocessors listed at /subprocessors — hosting, database, payments, messaging and AI providers — each under contract and on our instructions. It also reaches the other party to a booking: a provider sees what they need to deliver the session, and a family sees the provider's profile and messages. We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to protect a child's safety, to comply with law, or to respond to a valid legal request.

6. Children

Sporv is for adults arranging youth-sports services. Children may not create accounts or submit information directly. A parent or guardian provides what is needed about an athlete. We do not use information about a child for targeted advertising, and we do not permit a subprocessor to train a model on it. A parent may access, correct, or delete a child's information, or refuse further collection, by emailing support@sporv.ai. We verify the requester's relationship to the child before acting. If we learn we have collected a child's information without the consent required, we delete it.

7. Safety disclosures

Where something raises a genuine concern about a child's welfare, we may pass the relevant information to an adult responsible for that child, or to the proper authorities. We will not stay silent about a safety risk to preserve privacy.

8. Retention

We keep account and booking records while the account is open and afterwards only as long as needed for tax, accounting, dispute and safety obligations. Messages tied to a booking are kept with that booking. When a retention period ends, data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

9. Your rights

You may ask for a copy of your data, correction of it, or its deletion, and you may object to particular processing. Email support@sporv.ai. We will verify who you are before acting, and we will not treat you differently for asking.

10. Security

Access is restricted by authentication and database-level authorisation rules. Payment card data is handled by Stripe and never reaches our systems. No service is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected users promptly if a breach requires it.

11. Changes

We will update this notice as the service changes and will revise the date at the top. Where a change materially affects how we use personal data, we will give notice before it takes effect.
