What to expect

We aim to reply to support email within one business day. Safety reports are read the same day. We are a small team in Chicago, and we would rather tell you plainly that something will take time than leave you guessing — if a request needs longer, we will say so and say why.

If it is urgent and about a child's immediate safety, do not wait for us. Contact your local emergency services first, then tell us at safety@sporv.ai so we can act on the account.

Before you write in

Two things that make a booking question much faster to answer:

  • The email address on your Sporv account.
  • The booking reference, or the provider name and session date.

Please do not send payment card numbers. We never need them — card details are handled by Stripe and never reach Sporv.

Refunds and cancellations

Most money questions are answered by the policy itself, including the cancellation windows and how long a refund takes to appear: /refunds. If your situation is not covered there, write in and we will look at it.

Providers and coaches

Questions about listings, payouts, background checks or your account also go to support@sporv.ai. Payout timing is set by Stripe once a payout is initiated; we can tell you when it was sent and what it covered.
