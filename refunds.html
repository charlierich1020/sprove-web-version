Refunds & Cancellations

Last updated 24 August 2026 · Sporv Inc., Chicago, IL · support@sporv.ai

This page explains when a payment is refunded, how cancellations work, and how long money takes to move. It applies to every booking made through Sporv.

1. What you are paying for

A booking buys a specific session, at a stated time, with a stated provider. The price shown at checkout is the price charged. Sporv's platform fee is taken from the provider's payout and itemised there; it is never billed to a family separately.

2. Cancellation by a family

Every listing carries a cancellation policy, and the policy in force at the moment you book is copied onto your booking. A provider changing their policy later does not change the terms of a booking already made.

When you cancelWhat is refunded
More than 24 hours before the session starts100% of the amount paid
Less than 24 hours before the session starts50% of the amount paid
After the session has started, or no-showNo refund

These are the default terms. A listing may set a more generous policy, and where it does, the listing's policy applies. The exact terms for your booking are shown before you pay and again on the booking itself.

3. Cancellation by a provider

If a provider cancels a session for any reason, you receive a full refund, regardless of timing and regardless of the listing's policy. The same applies if a provider does not appear, or if Sporv removes a provider from the marketplace before a session you have booked.

4. Weather, illness and safety

A session cancelled for weather, facility closure, or a safety decision by the provider is treated as a provider cancellation and refunded in full. If you cannot attend because your athlete is unwell, contact the provider first: many will reschedule rather than refund, and rescheduling is usually the better outcome for a young athlete.

5. How to cancel

Cancel from the booking itself in your account, or email support@sporv.ai with the booking reference. A cancellation takes effect when we record it, not when it is sent, so cancel as early as you can if you are near a policy boundary.

6. How long a refund takes

Refunds are issued to the original payment method through Stripe, our payment processor. Sporv initiates the refund promptly once a cancellation is recorded. The money then moves on the card network's schedule, which is typically 5–10 business days and is outside our control. You will see the refund as a credit from Sporv, not as a reversal of the original charge.

7. Disputes and chargebacks

If something has gone wrong, email support@sporv.ai before raising a chargeback. We can usually resolve it faster than a card issuer can, and a chargeback on a session that was delivered may be contested with the booking record, the cancellation policy snapshot, and the provider's account of the session.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this page. A change applies to bookings made after it is published; it does not alter the terms copied onto a booking you have already made.
